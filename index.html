About me

I enjoy understanding people, exploring ideas and solving problems.

I came to marketing from finance. After a Bachelor of Commerce in Finance and Accountancy at Christ University, I worked in banking and global recruitment operations, where I got used to handling numbers and tight deadlines. Along the way I realised I was more curious about why people buy things than about the numbers alone.

That curiosity took me to an MSc in Marketing at the University of Manchester as a Global Futures Scholarship recipient. Since then I've done an SEO internship, run a controlled experiment in R and Python, and started building my own automation tool with n8n and APIs.

I like learning by building. Whether I'm analysing a dataset, working through a brand problem or wiring up an AI-powered workflow, I want to understand what works, why it works and how it could be better.
